# Script draft: Anatomy of a Traceable Scene

Editorial draft only. Approved narration lives in `scenes.json`, one scene at a time.

## Cold open / question
Every scene makes a promise: what you see and hear is backed by something real.

## Stakes and context
A claim is tracked separately from its source. It stays unverified until a named person reviews it.

## Investigation
Every visual carries its paperwork: creator, licence, and a checksum that catches a changed file.

## Resolution
Nothing publishes itself. People approve the script, the visuals and the final export.

## Source and visual notes
All four graphics are original SVGs produced by `scripts/build_demo_assets.py`. The sources are files in this repository.
