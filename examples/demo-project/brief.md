# Brief: Anatomy of a Traceable Scene

**Example data.** This demo project tests the pipeline. It is not a real episode.

## Central question

What has to be true before a scene is allowed into a documentary?

## Why it matters now

It shows the minimum chain this pipeline enforces: narration, then claim, then source, then a human review, with an asset record and checksum for every visual.

## Audience and promise

Anyone working on the pipeline. They should understand the data contracts in about 60 seconds of video.

## Known risks

- The claims refer only to files in this repository. They are deliberately left `unverified`, because no human has reviewed them yet.
- No narration audio exists yet. Narration is added in Phase 5.
