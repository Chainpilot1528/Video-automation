## 1. The narration voice: "Reckoner"

You asked for a narration voice like the one on *The Corporate Autopsy*. Cloning that narrator was declined, because it would be impersonation and against YouTube's rules. Instead, the pipeline matches the **style**:

- **Reference audio:** kept as `references/ANNT/ANNT.mp3`. It's read-only, private and never committed, and it's used only for listening and measurements. See `references/ANNT/README.md`.
- **What was measured:** adult male, about 133 Hz pitch, about 165 words/min, -20.5 LUFS, with music under the voice.
- **How the pipeline matches it:**
  - Kokoro `am_liam`, slowed to speed 0.87.
  - The **documentary** sound: warmth, a clearer voice, gentle compression and a limiter.
  - A **tone-matching EQ** measured against ANNT, which cut the tone difference from 5.4 dB to 2.5 dB.
- **Named "Reckoner"** (20 Oct): `--preset reckoner`. `--preset annt` still works and sounds identical.

```bash
.venv/bin/pipeline narration build projects/<project> --preset reckoner
```

## 2. Music bed and pacing

- **Original background music:** `music pad` makes a soft ambient pad from scratch. It uses no samples, so the rights are clean.
  - `music set` attaches it to a project.
  - It gets quieter automatically while the narrator speaks (ducking).
  - It sits about 10 dB under the voice in pauses.
- **Per-scene pacing:** each scene can have its own speed (`narration_speed`) and a pause before the cut (`hold_after_seconds`).
- **The Mathematics of Success, preview 5:** 2:12, -20.5 LUFS. That matches ANNT's loudness.
