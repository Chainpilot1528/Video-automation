# Brief: Your Brain Is Better at Defending You Than Improving You

## Central question

Why do capable people stop learning from failure, and what changes when they start questioning themselves?

## Format

A reflective story essay told through "Alex", an anonymous everyman. Narration is the creator's own text. Visuals are Pexels stock footage only, with no text slides or graphics.

## Audience and promise

Ambitious viewers who see themselves in Alex. They leave with one idea: being wrong is an event, not an identity.

## Known risks

- It's an essay with interpretive statements about how minds work, not factual reporting. No statistics or studies are claimed, so there's no claim ledger.
- Stock clips show real people. Each needs a people and trademark check before publication.
